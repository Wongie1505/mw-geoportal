Privacy Notice

Last updated: 23 November 2026

What we collect

When you register, we collect your name, email address, organisation and role. Your password is stored in encrypted form by our authentication provider, Supabase. We also keep records of logins, dataset requests, downloads and uploads, which we use for security and auditing.

Why we collect it

To create and run your account, to handle access requests, and to keep the site secure.

Who can see it

Your data is stored in Supabase. Only the project team and the people administering the site can see account details and audit records.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it outside the project unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account exists. Audit records are kept for 12 months.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data by contacting the project team. Deleting your account removes your personal data, except audit records that must be kept for security reasons.

Cookies

The site stores only what it needs to keep you signed in.
