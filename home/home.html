About the Geoportal

The Malawi Geoportal is a shared place for Malawian departments to publish, discover and request spatial data. Every dataset is described in the catalog, so you can see what it covers and who owns it before you use it.

Public datasets are open to everyone. Restricted datasets need approval: you send a request, and the department that owns the data decides. Staff with an account can also contribute datasets and manage requests from their dashboard.
