About the Malawi Geoportal

The Malawi Geoportal is a student project built by the GIS class of 2026 at Malawi University of Science and Technology (MUST) under the supervision of Harineck Tholo.

What we built

The Geoportal is a shared place where government departments can publish spatial data, and where anyone can find it. The dataset catalog lists each dataset with its description, owner and access level, and every dataset has its own page with an interactive map.

Public datasets can be downloaded directly. Restricted datasets need an access request, which the department that owns the data reviews. Department staff can upload datasets as GeoJSON, Shapefile or GeoTIFF files. Administrators manage departments and can review an audit log of activity on the site.
